## Featured Project

### 📊 HR Employee Attrition — Diagnostic EDA
> Why are employees leaving? That's the question this project answers.

A full exploratory data analysis of 14,999 employee records. I cleaned the dataset, fixed broken data types, handled missing values, and built 10 visualisations covering satisfaction, workload, salary, performance, tenure, and department-level patterns.

**Key findings:**
- Attrition rate of **23.8%** — nearly double the industry benchmark
- Both overworked *and* underworked employees are leaving (U-shaped workload curve)
- High performers are quitting too, not just poor performers
- Only **2%** of staff were promoted in 5 years — the biggest retention problem

**Tools used:** Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, Plotly, Streamlit
